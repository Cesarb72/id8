## Filter

A stop shaper is any reachable surface that can replace, substitute, reorder, drop, insert, repair, select a different candidate, change visible role, change the shown route, rebuild visible route identity, fall back to another route or stop, display a stale or alternate route as current, or authorize a different route than the assessed route.

A consumer transports, formats, renders, or labels already approved engine truth without changing stop identity, role, order, route identity, or authority.
